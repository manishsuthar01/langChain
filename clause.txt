SECTION 1 — CONFIDENTIALITY 1.1 The Employee shall maintain strict
confidentiality regarding all proprietary information, trade secrets,
source code, customer lists, business strategies, financial records, and
internal documentation belonging to the Company. 1.2 Confidential
Information shall not be disclosed to any third party without prior
written consent from the Company. 1.3 The confidentiality obligations
under this clause shall survive termination of employment for a period
of five (5) years.

SECTION 2 — NON-DISCLOSURE 2.1 The Receiving Party agrees not to
disclose any Confidential Information received from the Disclosing
Party. 2.2 Disclosure is permitted only to employees, advisors, or
contractors with a legitimate need to know. 2.3 Any unauthorized
disclosure shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

SECTION 3 — TERMINATION WITHOUT NOTICE 3.1 The Company may terminate the
employment of an Employee without prior notice in cases involving gross
misconduct, fraud, theft, or willful negligence. 3.2 Gross misconduct
includes unauthorized disclosure of confidential data, harassment,
violence, or severe policy violations. 3.3 Upon termination under this
clause, the Employee shall immediately return all Company property.

SECTION 4 — TERMINATION WITH NOTICE 4.1 Either Party may terminate this
Agreement by providing thirty (30) days written notice. 4.2 Salary and
benefits shall continue during the notice period unless otherwise
agreed. 4.3 Payment in lieu of notice may be provided at the Company’s
discretion.

SECTION 5 — PAYMENT TERMS 5.1 The Client agrees to pay all invoices
within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt. 5.2 Late payments shall
incur interest at the rate of 1.5% per month. 5.3 All fees are exclusive
of applicable taxes unless expressly stated.

SECTION 6 — REFUND POLICY 6.1 Refund requests must be submitted within
seven (7) days of purchase. 6.2 Refunds shall not apply to customized
services or completed milestones. 6.3 Approved refunds shall be
processed within ten (10) business days.

SECTION 7 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY 7.1 All work product, source code,
designs, and deliverables created under this Agreement shall remain the
exclusive property of the Company unless otherwise agreed in writing.
7.2 The Contractor assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work
product to the Company.

SECTION 8 — DATA PRIVACY 8.1 Both Parties shall comply with all
applicable data protection laws. 8.2 Personal data shall only be
processed for legitimate business purposes. 8.3 Reasonable technical and
organizational safeguards shall be implemented to protect personal data.

SECTION 9 — FORCE MAJEURE 9.1 Neither Party shall be liable for delay or
failure caused by events beyond reasonable control. 9.2 Such events
include natural disasters, war, terrorism, labor strikes, or
governmental actions.

SECTION 10 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION 10.1 Any dispute arising under this
Agreement shall first be resolved through good faith negotiation. 10.2
If unresolved, disputes shall proceed to arbitration. 10.3 The seat of
arbitration shall be Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.

SECTION 11 — EMPLOYEE MISCONDUCT 11.1 Employee misconduct includes
insubordination, repeated absenteeism, theft, fraud, workplace
harassment, and violation of company policies. 11.2 Serious misconduct
may result in disciplinary action including immediate dismissal.

SECTION 12 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT 12.1 The Service Provider
guarantees 99.9% uptime excluding scheduled maintenance. 12.2 Critical
incidents must receive an initial response within one (1) hour.

SECTION 13 — WARRANTY 13.1 The Vendor warrants that all deliverables
will conform to agreed specifications. 13.2 Warranty claims must be
raised within ninety (90) days of delivery.

SECTION 14 — INDEMNIFICATION 14.1 Each Party agrees to indemnify and
hold harmless the other Party against claims arising from breach,
negligence, or misconduct. 14.2 Indemnification obligations survive
termination.

SECTION 15 — GOVERNING LAW 15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and
construed in accordance with the laws of India. 15.2 Courts located in
Rajasthan shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

SECTION 16 — RESIGNATION 16.1 An Employee intending to resign must
provide at least thirty (30) days prior written notice. 16.2 Failure to
serve the notice period may result in salary deductions.

SECTION 17 — CONTRACT BREACH 17.1 Failure to perform obligations under
this Agreement constitutes a breach. 17.2 The non-breaching Party may
terminate the Agreement upon written notice.

SECTION 18 — SECURITY INCIDENT 18.1 Any cybersecurity breach, data leak,
or unauthorized access incident must be reported within twenty-four (24)
hours. 18.2 Immediate remediation steps shall be initiated.

SECTION 19 — FREELANCER ENGAGEMENT 19.1 Freelancers are independent
contractors and shall not be deemed employees. 19.2 Freelancers are
responsible for their own taxes and statutory obligations.

SECTION 20 — LIABILITY LIMITATION 20.1 Neither Party shall be liable for
indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. 20.2 Total liability
shall not exceed the total fees paid under this Agreement.
